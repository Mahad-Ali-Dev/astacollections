# Asta Collections: technical SEO and UI audit (3 Nov 2026)

Checked against the live site at https://astacollections.com, plus the code on `main` at `fc36a54`.

## Fixed in branch `claude/seo-ui`

| # | Problem (live) | Impact | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Every page's canonical pointed to the home page.** The root layout set `alternates.canonical: "/"` and no page overrode it. | Google was told all 158 products and 14 collections are duplicates of `/`. This is the biggest reason the site gets little non-brand traffic. | Removed it. Every indexable page now sets a self-referencing canonical through `pageMeta()` in `src/lib/seo.ts`. |
| 2 | Cart, checkout, login, register and account pages were `index, follow`. | Thin pages in the index. | `noindex, follow` via `privatePageMeta()`. Order-success and admin pages are noindex too. robots.txt no longer blocks them, so Google can see the noindex and drop any it already has. |
| 3 | **Soft 404s / hidden collections**: `/products/does-not-exist` and `/category/bridal-sets` return HTTP 200 (with a Next-added `noindex`), and hidden (inactive) collections and products still rendered as normal indexable pages. | Hidden collections stay indexed; dead URLs show as "Soft 404" in GSC. | Missing **and** inactive products and collections now call `notFound()` (in `generateMetadata` too), so they render the 404 page with `noindex`. The status stays 200 because `src/app/(shop)/loading.tsx` streams the shell first. A hard 404 would need that loading skeleton removed from product and collection routes, a UX trade-off left for later. Google drops `noindex` pages either way. |
| 4 | Site copy, meta description and the OG image promised **bridal, kundan, pearl and necklace sets**. The FAQ said pieces are "gold-plated brass, sterling silver, kundan, freshwater pearls". The catalogue is 100% stainless steel. | Mismatched intent leads to bounces. It's also a trust and consumer-protection problem. | Rewrote site description, home banners, footer, search suggestions and FAQ to describe stainless steel truthfully. |
| 5 | Links to non-existent `/category/bridal-sets` and `/category/necklaces` in the footer, home banners, search overlay, empty-cart page and header fallback. | Broken internal links. | Repointed them to real collections. |
| 6 | Stale money copy. FAQ and Shipping said "Rs. 250 COD advance" and "free shipping above Rs. 5,000". The cart progress bar used 5,000. The catalogue banner and home pillars said 5,000. The product shipping tab said "Rs. 250 advance". The settings say COD has no advance, delivery is Rs. 250, and it's free over Rs. 3,000. | Shoppers were told to spend more than needed, and told COD needs a bank transfer when it doesn't. | Added `src/lib/store-policy.ts` (display only) and wired the trust strip, FAQ, Shipping, cart, catalogue and checkout header to it. Checkout and order pricing are untouched. |
| 7 | Generic titles ("Rings", "Earrings", "Shop All Jewellery"); meta descriptions like "Beautiful earrings collection". | No keyword targeting. | Keyword-led titles, descriptions and H1s per collection (`src/lib/collection-copy.ts`). Product titles gain "Price in Pakistan" when there's room, and descriptions end with the live PKR price and COD. |
| 8 | Home page H1 was the rotating hero slide title. | Weak, changing H1. | Hero titles are now `h2`. The home page has one H1 ("Stainless steel jewellery, delivered across Pakistan") plus an intro linking the six main collections. |
| 9 | Structured data: a `Store` with a "Rs. 1,000 – Rs. 35,000" price range and opening hours an online store doesn't have. Product `Offer` had no shipping or returns. | Wrong facts; missed merchant-listing enhancements. | Organization is now `OnlineStore` with `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`. Product offers include `shippingDetails` (live fee, free-delivery threshold, 1–2 + 3–5 day timing), the return policy and `material`. Collections get `BreadcrumbList`, `ItemList` and `FAQPage` markup. JSON-LD is now escaped. |
| 10 | AggregateRating from bulk-imported reviews (951, most added via the admin CSV importer). | Google requires genuine customer reviews for star snippets. | **Owner confirmed (3 Nov 2026) the imported reviews are real buyer reviews**, so product JSON-LD keeps AggregateRating and the latest 5 reviews per product, counting only that product's approved reviews. If they ever import reviews that aren't from buyers, filter them out of the `groupBy` in `products/[slug]/page.tsx`. |
| 11 | Collection pages had no breadcrumbs, intro copy, FAQs or links to sibling collections. Category names in the DB are slugs ("couple-rings", "Hand-Cuff", "Hormony"). | Thin pages, weak internal linking. | Added visible breadcrumbs, intro, FAQs and "Related collections". Display names come from the copy file without touching the DB. Product pages link to "Shop all \<collection>". |
| 12 | Sitemap listed `/login`, `/register` and `/track-order`, and had no images. robots.txt had a Googlebot group that silently replaced the `*` rules, plus a non-standard `Host`. | Noise. | The sitemap now lists only rankable pages and includes up to 4 image URLs per product. robots.txt is simplified. |
| 13 | Site search was case-sensitive (Postgres `contains`), so "ring" found nothing called "Ring". | This is the search the `WebSite` SearchAction advertises. | `mode: "insensitive"`. Search and filter URLs are `noindex`. |
| 14 | No `llms.txt`. | | `/llms.txt` is generated live from collections, price range and policies. |
| 15 | Accessibility: the rose-gold accent `#BE8474` on white is about 3.1:1, used for small text and white-on-accent sale badges. Links had no visible keyboard focus. The hero slide dots were 1 px tall buttons. | WCAG AA failures. | Accent deepened to `hsl(13 40% 44%)` (about 5.4:1). Added a global `:focus-visible` outline. Dots are now 24 px tap targets. |
| 16 | On phones the invisible "Quick Add" button sat over the bottom of each product photo, so tapping the photo could silently add the item to the bag. Product names were cut to one line. | Accidental adds. | Quick Add only renders on hover-capable devices and shows on keyboard focus. Names wrap to two lines. |
| 17 | The main product image had an empty `alt` when the DB `alt` was `""` (all 714 images). | Image search. | Falls back to the product name. Decorative hero and banner backgrounds now use `alt=""`. |
| 18 | `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` with a trailing slash would produce `https://host//path`. | | Normalised in `SITE.url`. |
| 19 | No content beyond product pages. | Nothing to rank for "how to" and gift searches. | Added `/guides` with 5 fact-checked starter guides (see SEO-PLAN §4). |

## Not fixed: needs the owner, or out of scope

1. **`www.astacollections.com` has no valid TLS certificate** (`SEC_E_WRONG_PRINCIPAL`). Anyone typing www gets a browser security error. In Vercel go to Project → Settings → Domains → add `www.astacollections.com` and set it to **redirect (308) to `astacollections.com`**.
2. **Announcement bar and marquee text are admin content** and still say "Free delivery over Rs. 5,000". Change them in Admin → Site content to match the Rs. 3,000 setting.
3. **About page claims** ("skilled artisans across Pakistan", "hand-inspected", "premium gold-plating that doesn't fade", "hypoallergenic settings") and the product badge "Quality first · Hand-inspected", plus the header's "Made in Pakistan", aren't something I can verify from the catalogue. The products are stainless steel, so "gold-plating that doesn't fade" may not be accurate. Keep them only if true.
4. **Public API leaks cost price.** `GET /api/products` returns every field, including `costPrice` (your margin), to anyone. This is a separate fix: select only public fields.
5. **WELCOME10** on the home and catalogue offer timers: confirm the coupon exists and is active.
7. Product descriptions and meta titles are admin-written and mostly good. A few have typos ("hexagnal golden stainless stell"). Edit them in Admin → Products.
8. Category hero images on mobile are full-width `priority` images. Fine now, but keep uploads under about 300 KB.
